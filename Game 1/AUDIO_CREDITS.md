# Audio assets — 3D Combat Mode 1.1

The original twelve-file 1.1 set used original, locally generated synthesis, not sampled recordings. Eleven of those files remain; `time-resume.ogg` is now a mix of Kenney CC0 recordings credited in `ASSET_CREDITS.md`. Their source waveforms were generated by `tools/generate_combat_audio.py` on 2026-10-12 and encoded as mono 48 kHz Opus with `tools/encode_combat_audio.sh`. Source, maker and license: Codex-generated original audio for this repository; no third-party recording, voice, music track, or external URL is embedded. The source scripts are included so the files can be reproduced (Python NumPy and FFmpeg required).

The audio manager begins after a start/resume click, exposes master/music/ambience/effects and mute, and retains preferences. The active mapping now mixes Kenney recordings and the existing generated time/ambient cues described below. Browser decoding/playback and perceived speaker quality are separate checks. Existing user-supplied voices retain their previous licensing status; no new MyInstants or anime fragment is introduced.

## Foley update — 12 October 2026

Core footsteps, light/heavy hits, enemy damage, dodge cloth, swing, guard and preparation now use Kenney CC0 recordings. Six Arcade baselines are also replaced. See ASSET_CREDITS.md for exact sources and edits. The original, locally generated synthesis files remain for ambient/music/time cues and as archived existing assets; their presence does not mean all active events use them.

The recorded time-resume mix also replaces Arcade `sfx-rebirth.mp3`. Core recording counts are now seven replaced Arcade files, sixteen preserved source Oggs and one derived arena cue. The generator scripts reproduce the historical synthesized set, not the newer recorded release cue.
