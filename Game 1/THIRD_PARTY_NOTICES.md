## Character references and audio

The procedural 2D Stand illustrations and cosmic scenes in `src/character-director.js` and
`src/cosmic-director.js` were newly drawn in code. They were informed by the user's
reference images; no external reference images or anime footage were copied into these files.

Characters, recordings, performances, names and visual designs from JoJo's Bizarre Adventure
may be protected by third-party rights. This repository's existing JoJo-related sound files
were present before Cinematic Rebuild 3.0. No new user-supplied MP3 was published in this
rebuild. The new Made in Heaven and Time Resume voice recordings can be selected for private,
local preview from the Settings file pickers. Obtain appropriate permissions before any public
distribution of those recordings or other protected material.
